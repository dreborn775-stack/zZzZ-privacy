zZzZ 💤 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the zZzZ 💤 Discord bot ("the Bot") handles information when it is used in a Discord server.

1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on the features enabled by a server administrator, the Bot may process Discord identifiers and server information needed to provide its features, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, usernames, and command interactions.

Features such as AFK and economy may store information required for those features, such as an AFK status/reason or an in-bot coin balance.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to operate and improve the Bot's features, including moderation, verification, autoroles, AFK status, tickets, welcome messages, economy commands, fun commands, and server utilities.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information processed by the Bot is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties except where necessary to operate a feature or where required by law.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data should be retained only for as long as needed for the relevant Bot feature. Server administrators can request removal of Bot-stored server data. Users can also ask a server administrator to request deletion of information associated with a Bot feature.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information under Discord's own policies. This policy applies to the zZzZ 💤 Bot, not Discord itself.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect Bot-stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy or data questions, contact the person responsible for the zZzZ 💤 Bot through the Discord server where the Bot is installed.
